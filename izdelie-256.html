§ 1 Objective

Build one instrumented test facility and one propulsion article; run a disciplined test campaign with a pre-test prediction, stated uncertainty, and honest correlation; present the result as a deadpan Soviet experimental-program homage. Audience: aerospace engineers and executives (NASA, Airbus, SpaceX) and skilled hands-on traditionalists. Desired reaction: "why does this exist" → "wait, this is real engineering." Governing aesthetic law: crude design, precise execution — the gap is the joke. Secondary objective: the program doubles as the Chief Designer's introduction-to-CNC curriculum — real machined parts with zero-stakes fallbacks. Tertiary objective, added this revision: the finished article must read as gorgeous to non-engineers using only approved cold-side and material means — beauty is achieved through metallurgy, mixture, and lighting, never through structural risk.

§ 2 Program architecture — two articles, one gate

Common infrastructure begins immediately; the article is selected at a formal gate, so the open land question costs zero schedule.

Article 256Б · Primary intent

Linear valveless pulsejet, Lockwood/SNECMA-family geometry. 256Б uses a Western valveless architecture; the Soviet treatment is a design-bureau homage, not a claim of engine lineage. Hot section in 304 stainless, polished before first fire — the engine's own heat then lays down permanent straw-gold-purple-blue temper coloration graded along the tube (the single highest-yield gorgeousness decision in the program; ask the shop about TIG for stainless when booking — mild steel remains the cost/availability fallback). Propane, ~1–2 kgf mean thrust, ~100–250 Hz, ~140 dB, hot section glows in daylight. Requires authorized drive-out test land.

Standing prohibitions: no garage, driveway, or municipal firing, ever, for any duration, under any rationalization — 140 dB is indifferent to intent. And no transparent combustion section on Mk I: the tube wall is the chamber, so "see-through housing" means a quartz combustion vessel — a real but genuinely difficult lab-engineering problem (thermal-mismatch sealing on a structure vibrating at 200 Hz; failure mode is hot glass on a running engine). It is filed with its own designation, Изделие 256С (стеклянный), for a future plan, on a second engine. A transparent outer shroud is equally denied: plastic melts in the radiant field and there is no cool place to put a window on a machine that is entirely hot section. Mk I is opaque by design and glows anyway.

Article 256Э · Contingency, held without shame

~70 mm commercial EDF core with printed inlet/duct/nozzle variant families and machined interfaces. Thrust-per-kW campaign, garage-testable nightly. LiPo charge/storage discipline is this article's principal fire risk and gets a written protocol. 256Э is also the designated exit door: if, at any point, the propane sections of this program read as dread rather than respect, the electric article delivers ~80% of the program at RC-hobby hazard levels, and taking that door is a program decision, not a defeat.

The Gate — end of week 3 · calls begin week 1

Criterion unchanged and strict — explicit confirmation from the landowner or club AND the authority having jurisdiction that this specific propane-fed static propulsion test is permissible under current fire restrictions. "It's BLM land" establishes nothing; a campfire permit is not evidence. Site authorization is the dominant critical path — above welding.

Site A (strongly preferred): an AMA club within day-trip range that already hosts turbine/pulsejet operation. Site B: private rural land with written permission and an AHJ check. From San Mateo: Central Valley and East Bay hinterlands, 1.5–3 hours; sessions are planned expeditions. Firm authorization → 256Б; otherwise → 256Э without mourning, 256Б held in reserve. Both bolt to the same stand. The contingency does not get a contingency: if welding falls through — second shop, then 256Э. The threaded-pipe improvisation remains deleted.

§ 3 Workstream FACILITY weeks 1–4 · article-independent

Thrust stand: fixed base → load cell behind a radiant heat shield → sliding engine carriage → article. One fixed mount, one sliding mount; slider travel sized by Analysis C with margin. Load cell by requirement, not preselection: capacity chosen after establishing maximum credible stand load with overload margin; mechanical travel stops and overload protection independent of the sensor; purchase deferred until stand geometry is fixed. Fastener retention by engineering: proper torque and preload first; locking features by location and temperature; no nylocs in heat-soak zones (this now explicitly includes the shroud region, §7); safety wire only where hardware and failure mode justify it.

Carvera apprenticeship ladder — five real parts in difficulty order, each with a catalog fallback (~$30 insurance kit). All flat 2.5D on the standalone 3-axis machine — the ladder contains zero rotary parts by design. When asked "what did you machine," the answer is a parts list, not a sign.

  2. 256-02Engine support bracket (the program's L-bracket)
  3. 256-03Sliding expansion support (first slot)
  4. 256-04Control panel (pockets, holes, engraving, chamfers)
  5. 256-05Brass data plate (the reward)

P1S role: welding fixtures, V-blocks, drill templates, interface fit-check sections, knobs, blower adapter, crate stencils, and (new) the cutaway teaching model, §7. No full 1:1 mockup. All plastics ≥30 cm from hot metal.

Electrical doctrine, non-negotiable: safety chain entirely electromechanical — ПУСК master-arm (enormous, guarded, functional: physical series contact in the low-voltage supply) → held dead-man → АВАРИЙНЫЙ СТОП breaking everything → NC fuel solenoid (256Б) or ESC arm/throttle-cut (256Э). The Arduino (HX711, MAX31855, V/I) reads everything and gates nothing; behavior must be identical with the microcontroller unplugged mid-run. Software observes; copper decides. No PCB, no Pi, no state-machine theater. Perfboard is period-correct — and now deliberately beautiful perfboard, per §7's clear-bay option.

§ 4 Workstream ARTICLE

256Б: proven published valveless planset, dimensions locked week 1, zero geometric freelancing — the engine is a tuned resonator. 304 stainless exhaust tube and bought reducer cones (§2), ~6 welds, outsourced to a shop booked in week 1; builder arrives with printed fixtures holding final alignment and a weld drawing — buying minutes of arc time, not a builder. Shop may also cut tubes to length in the same visit (see §6, grinder demotion).

Fuel train: cylinder valve → LP-rated regulator → needle valve → NC solenoid → injector (drilled copper baseline; brass orifice as Carvera flair). Every component rated for LP-gas service; hose short, heat-routed; final parts list reviewed at a genuine propane supplier's counter before first fuel. Mixture configurations, logged: lean/best-thrust for the test campaign, and режим «ПАРАД» — a documented rich setting (needle valve past best thrust) producing a long pulsing exhaust plume and visible intake flame for demonstrations; parade runs carry a deepened downstream exclusion zone and appear as a configuration in the test table like everything else. Ignition: automotive coil, continuous spark during start; piezo backup; spare plugs. Start air: leaf blower with sacrificial printed adapter — the contrast is preserved by decree.

256Э: reputable 70 mm EDF and matched ESC; arming plug on the hardwired chain; variant families = nozzle exit area sweep (~85–100% of fan area) and inlet lip geometry, three articles each; optional momentum-source CFD credited only for predicted deltas, never absolutes.

§ 5 Workstream TEST

Four calculations, all hardware-affecting: (A) first-order acoustic estimate with open-end corrections, published pre-test as a predicted band with stated sound-speed assumptions; (B) one-hour stand modal sanity check (eigenfrequency run or cold tap test) confirming no structural mode in the forcing band — after which the report may lawfully state structural design methodology: sufficiently Soviet amounts of steel; (C) thermal growth sizing the slider; (D) a ten-minute temperature estimate for any 6061 mounted near the shroud region (§7) — aluminum softens by ~200–300°C, so the rings either sit at demonstrably cool stations, clear the calculation, or become brushed stainless. Philosophy: calculate when the answer affects hardware; never simulate because Ansys has buttons.

Baseline measurement set (three, and no more): quasi-static time-averaged thrust (HX711 at 10–80 S/s is explicitly not a dynamic force transducer; known-weight calibration before and after every session; uncertainty and repeatability reported). Skin temperature at one consistent location, inconel-sheathed K-type ×2 (the first is a consumable) — read in both shroud-on and shroud-off configurations once T-03 exists, since a radiation shield reflects heat back at the wall. Dominant frequency via phone spectrogram at distance — frequency only, never SPL; clipping honestly reported. Stretch (L4): fuel mass via luggage scale, contingent on demonstrated resolution; test duration is set by the article and site, never by a $15 scale. 256Э analog: thrust-per-kW.

Graduated success:

  • L1Ten seconds self-sustained / first instrumented run — the project fundamentally works.
  • L2Repeatable starts.
  • L3Full baseline dataset.
  • L4Controlled campaign, one variable at a time, runs logged 256-001 onward.
Crown deliverable, one sentence: "Predicted 190–215 Hz; measured 178; −8% from band edge; attributed to mean-gas-temperature overestimate."

§ 6 Test operations, safety & beginner doctrine 256Б

Control hierarchy: distance → remote operation → exposure time → PPE. Established exclusion zone (deepened downstream for ПАРАД runs); operator at maximum dead-man lead; minimum run durations; double hearing protection as the last layer, never the plan — one unprotected close exposure can be permanent.

Minimum hot-fire crew: two. TEST DIRECTOR (dead-man in hand) and SAFETY OFFICER — briefed on shutdown, fuel isolation, extinguisher/water stations, exclusion watch, independent abort authority. The killer scenario is mundane: one person cannot fight a grass ember and close a cylinder valve simultaneously. Cold work remains single-operator.

Weather gate: red-flag warnings or burn restrictions scrub the session, no debate; water staged beyond the extinguisher; bare dirt/gravel; nobody downwind. Hot-work separation: no spark-producing work in the fuel/test area, ever; tuning loop = shut down → isolate fuel → cool → cold-work area → modify → return. Cold commissioning before the article travels: soapy-water leak check (bottle outdoors), spark bench test, load-cell calibration, dead-man/E-stop verification — the article arrives at the range with exactly one untested behavior. Post-run: no-touch cooldown; gloves after; metal stays branding-hot long after it stops glowing — the classic beginner injury is touching it twenty minutes early.

Beginner doctrine (new, binding — the Chief Designer has built only plastic until now): The plan itself is the primary safety equipment; it protects only when followed precisely when it is annoying. Software habits — cheap, reversible, rapid iteration — do not transfer; improvisation under frustration is where hardware hurts people, and the field moment of "let me just try one thing" is hereby designated a forbidden sentence alongside its ancestor about 12 mm. Grinder demotion: the angle grinder is the highest injury-density tool in the program and is deferred — the weld/muffler shop cuts tubes to length in the booking visit; if a grinder is later acquired, first hour on clamped scrap, face shield, never freehand, never at the field. Calibration afternoon: before fabrication month ends, one session absorbing what normal looks and sounds like — a maker-space metal-shop intro, or ten minutes of the weld-shop's time watching you drill and deburr. Not buying skills; buying calibration. Exit door standing: §2's 256Э clause may be exercised at any time without ceremony.

§ 7 Workstream THEATER funded only after successful commissioning

Joke-placement doctrine unchanged — one joke, one home, descending volume: crate announced in English; artifact discovered in straight-faced Russian; report contains no joke at all, which is the joke.

Material palette (new, program-wide): material contrast reads as "expensive" to everyone; monochrome reads only to engineers. The engine itself stays honest bare metal — welds, plumbing, load path inspectable; no paint on the article, ever — its heat coloration is the finish, applied by the engine itself.

Report: one page, institute format, masthead as above, honest error bars, and the two-requirement verification matrix — R-001 extremely loud noise — VERIFIED BY TEST; R-002 modest amount of thrust — VERIFIED BY TEST. All requirements met. Program declared a complete success. Verbal deliverable: "It met all requirements." Final deliverables: the dusk video, the heat-rainbowed artifact, the report, the crate, the teaching kit.

§ 9 Excluded by decree

Turbine cores, compressor wheels, liquid fuel, custom fuel pumps, custom PCB, Raspberry Pi, FADEC theater, reacting-flow CFD, acoustic CFD, lathe acquisition, 4th-axis and laser modules (denied without prejudice), transparent combustion sections and quartz windows (filed as Изделие 256С, стеклянный, for a future plan), transparent outer shrouds in the radiant field, additional machinery of any kind, garage hot-fire under any rationalization, the threaded-pipe engine, field improvisation of every species, and every attractive idea that arrives after this sentence — each filed as Изделие 256В for the next five-year plan.

§ 10 Definition of finished

A real propulsion article, fabricated by the Chief Designer on his first hardware program, runs repeatably on a stand the Chief Designer machined; produces mean thrust, temperature, and frequency data with stated uncertainty; matches — or honestly misses, with attributed error — a pre-test prediction; is gorgeous at dusk to any human alive; and only then receives its uniform, its humorless nameplate, its two-requirement report, its teaching kit, and its loud-mouthed crate. The story afterward: "I wanted a small fabrication project, so naturally I established a Soviet propulsion design bureau in my garage."
